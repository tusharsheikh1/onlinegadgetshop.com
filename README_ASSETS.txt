These assets are extracted directly from visible pixels, without regeneration, background removal, upscaling, or retouching. Every extracted image was verified pixel-for-pixel against its source rectangle.

Product photos, product galleries, promotional banners, lifestyle scenes, editorial images, avatars, map illustrations and logo/header crops are grouped by screen and device. Pixel equality applies to each source crop. This does not promise a 100% identical implemented website: layout, typography, responsive behavior and text must be implemented separately. Larger originals and transparent isolated products cannot be recovered from this ZIP.
